ABOUT

Curious about how things work — and how they can work better.

George is a student maker interested in artificial intelligence, robotics, engineering, and human-centered problem solving. His projects begin with a practical question: what can be improved, simplified, or made more useful?

This site documents not only finished projects, but also the process behind them: ideas, sketches, code, prototypes, failed attempts, debugging, testing, and iteration.
