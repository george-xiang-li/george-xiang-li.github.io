sections:
  - heading: The Problem
    body: |
      Traditional toolboxes can be difficult to organize and search, especially when items are divided into many categories. Fixed storage can also make retrieval inconvenient for elderly users and children.
  - heading: The Idea
    body: |
      George explored whether voice control could make a storage system easier to use. The goal was to let a user request an item or category without needing to search manually.
  - heading: How It Works
    body: |
      The prototype combines an **UNO board**, a **voice recognition module**, a **servo motor**, compartmentalized storage, and an **RGB status light**. A spoken command is recognized and translated into a control action that moves the storage unit to the requested position.
  - heading: Materials & Components
    body: |
      - UNO board
      - Voice recognition module
      - Servo motor
      - RGB light
      - Dupont wires
      - Voltage regulator
      - Lithium battery
      - Wooden structural parts
  - heading: Human-Centered Design
    body: |
      The project was designed around accessibility and convenience. Voice interaction can reduce the need to search through multiple compartments and may make everyday storage easier for users whose hands are occupied or who find manual searching inconvenient.
